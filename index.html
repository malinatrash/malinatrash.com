About me

I'm a Go backend engineer who specialises in microservices and distributed systems. In 4+ years I've built and run 50+ Go services across fintech, e-government and B2B platforms: service boundaries and contracts, messaging, consistency, failure handling and observability.

Currently AI-First Senior Golang Engineer at Rocket Firm, working on two fintech projects: backend owner of One Limit, a fully online credit product at Bereke Bank, and author of the Halyk Travel backend from its first commit.

I work best where the cost of failure is high: money, personal data, legally binding signatures. That's why I build in reliability, observability and predictability from day one, not after the first incident.

My default is hexagonal architecture (ports & adapters): business logic knows nothing about transport, databases or vendors, so every integration can be swapped or mocked and the core stays easy to test.

I work AI-first: features, fixes and incident investigations go through Claude Code agents and custom skills, while I own the architecture, review and release.

    Halyk Travel · tours in the Halyk Bank super app

    Tour search, booking and payment inside the Halyk Bank super app. Started the backend from the first commit: 4 of 5 Go services (BFF, tour provider, payments, notifications) and the base of the shared library.

    • Architecture: gRPC microservices with a REST/OpenAPI edge; 64 BFF, 52 provider and 13 payment methods.
